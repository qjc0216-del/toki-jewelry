# TOKI Jewelry redesign direction

Applied guidance from:
- Anthropic `frontend-design` skill: distinctive subject-led visual direction, restrained motion, deliberate typography/layout, avoid template defaults.
- `avoid-ai-design` skill: remove generic AI UI patterns and overused decorative labels/numbering.
- Corey Haines `cro` skill: prioritize 5-second clarity, CTA hierarchy, scannability, visible pricing, trust and friction reduction.

## Direction
Young quiet luxury: clean, image-led, contemporary, tactile. Keep TOKI burgundy as an accent, not the dominant background. Use the final approved TOKI master logo asset exactly. Product names and prices are primary shopping information; price must remain obvious on desktop and mobile.

## Tokens
- Porcelain: #FCFAF7
- White: #FFFFFF
- Burgundy: #642D3A
- Espresso: #241C1A
- Rose: #D8B7B1
- Gold: #CBAA74

## Rules
- No numbered section decoration unless it encodes a real sequence.
- Avoid all-caps eyebrow labels on every section.
- One restrained page-load reveal only.
- Product price minimum 20px desktop / 18px mobile and medium-to-semibold weight.
- Primary CTA visible above the fold.
- Large photography and generous white space; fewer dark panels.
